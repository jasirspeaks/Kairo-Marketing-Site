About

Built by one seller, for sellers who carry a pipeline alone.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Kairo is a deal intelligence workspace — not a CRM, not a note-taker, not a coaching tool. It exists to answer one question: what am I missing on this deal, before it costs me?

Why Kairo exists

Every deal quietly accumulates risk between calls — a stakeholder who's gone dark, a concern that surfaced once and was never resolved, a champion whose enthusiasm has cooled. Full-cycle AEs, founder-led sellers, consultants, and agency owners feel this constantly, but rarely have a system built to catch it. CRMs track stages and activities. Note-takers transcribe conversations. Neither one tells you what's actually changing about a deal's risk profile over time.

Kairo was built to close that gap — treating the deal itself, not any single call, as the thing worth understanding.

What Kairo actually does

Every deal in Kairo is a continuously updated workspace. Calls, transcripts, and recordings are evidence that feed into it — not the end product themselves. Each new conversation updates Kairo's understanding of the deal: what's known, what's still missing, what the highest-priority risk is, and what to do next.

Kairo's core strength is tracking how risk evolves across multiple calls on the same deal, and surfacing patterns across your whole pipeline — not just reviewing a single conversation in isolation, which most tools already do well enough.

Where Kairo is today

Kairo is early-stage and actively developed. Pricing, features, and integrations are evolving quickly based on direct feedback from the people using it. If something feels rough or incomplete, it probably is — and that's exactly the kind of thing worth telling us about.
